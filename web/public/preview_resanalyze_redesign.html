// THE PIPELINE

One upload. Four passes.

Your resume is never sent to a model blindly. It's parsed, tagged, scored and rewritten in four deterministic passes — every claim traceable to the line it came from.

  1. PASS 01

    Parse & extract

    NER pulls every person, organization, skill and date out of the raw text.

  2. PASS 02

    Score

    ATS-style keyword density, readability and impact metrics computed per section.

  3. PASS 03

    Gap map

    Your skill set is diffed against the role's required stack, line by line.

  4. PASS 04

    Rewrite

    Weak bullets are reforged into measurable impact statements you can copy back.
